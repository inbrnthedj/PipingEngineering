# Pipe Stress
Stress is generated by a force>
$Stress = \frac{Force}{Area}$

As per ASME B31.3, the major forces that causes stress in a piping system are>
- Internal and External Pressure
- Temperature change (from installation to operating phases, both expansion and contraction)
- Weights (Pipe weight, fluid weight, rigid body weights, insulation weight)
- Ambient effects
- Dynamic forces (Wind, Seismic, vibration, impact, slug, surge. etc.)
- settlement / position
