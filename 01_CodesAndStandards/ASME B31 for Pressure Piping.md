# Pressure Piping
ASME B31 for Pressure Piping consists of:
- **B31.1**    Power Piping
- **B31.3**    Process Piping
- **B31.4**    Pipeline Transportation Systems for Liquid Hydrocarbons and Other Liquids
- **B31.5**    Refrigeration Piping
- **B31.8**    Gas Transportation and Distribution Piping Systems
- **B31.9**    Building Services Piping
- **B31.11**    Slurry Transportation Piping Systems
- **B31.12**    Hydrogen Piping and Pipelines

Here are the other sections that were published as independent codes but got withdrawn:
- **B31.2**     Fuel Gas Piping
- **B31.7**     Nuclear Power Piping

Here are the sections that were never published but got incorporated into B31.3:
- **B31.6**     Chemical Plant Piping
- **B31.10**    Cryogenic Piping

There are some cases that multiple parts of the ASME B31 series are applicable to a single plant. In those cases, it's the owner's responsibility to specify which section/s are applicable to a proposed piping specification.
