# Relation between ASME B31.1 and ASME BPVC

## ASME BPVC
It establishes rules governing the design, fabrication, and inspection of boilers, pressure vessels, and nuclear power plant components during construction. 

ASME BPVC Section I covers rules for construction of:
1. Power Boilers
2. Electric Boilers
3. Miniature Boilers
4. High-Temperature Water Boilers
5. Heat Recovery Steam Generators
6. Certain Fired Pressure Vessels

ASME BPVC covers:
- **Boiler Proper Piping (entirely under BPVC Section I):**
  - Superheaters, economizers, and other pressure parts connected directly to the boiler without intervening valves.
- **Boiler External Piping (BEP):**
  - Feedwater, main steam, vent, drain, blow-off, and chemical feed piping up to the jurisdictional limits.
  - *Note:* Design and construction follow **ASME B31.1**, but administration, Authorized Inspection, and Stamping follow **ASME BPVC Section I**.

### Certification Marks and Certificates of Authorization
**Certification marks** are required to be affixed to the product/piping with the following designators:
- **S** : Power Boiler Designator
- **A** : Boiler Assembly Designator
- **PP** : Pressure Piping Designator

These designators denote entities authorized to fabricate, install, and hydrostatically test boiler external piping under an ASME Quality Control System.

A Manufacturer's **Certificate of Authorization** is a document issued by ASME authorizing the use of the ASME certification mark and appropriate designator for a specified period and scope.

**Non-Boiler External Piping (NBEP)** is not covered by Section I of the ASME BPVC and is not subject to Section I ASME certification, AI inspection, or Code stamping.

---

## ASME B31.1
ASME B31.1 may not be required by law in every jurisdiction, but it is routinely mandated by contract for new construction. It is the Owner's responsibility to determine the specific B31 Code section to be used for Non-Boiler External Piping (NBEP).

### Editions to Use
Because B31.1 is updated every 2 years, the governing edition is typically:
- The latest edition issued at least **6 months prior to the original contract date** for the first phase of activity.
- Alternative Editions/Addenda if:
  - A specific agreement is made between contracting parties.
  - The regulatory body having jurisdiction mandates the use of a specific edition.
