# Scope of ASME B31.1

ASME B31.1 prescribes rules for the design, materials, fabrication, erection, examination, testing, and inspection of power piping systems.

### Typical Applications
1. **Plant & Facility Systems:**
   - Electric power generating stations
   - Institutional and industrial plants
   - Geothermal heating systems
   - Central and district heating and cooling systems
2. **Fluid Services & Materials:**
   - Steam, water, oil, gas, air, and other utility/process services
   - Metallic and nonmetallic piping (including lined piping)
   - All pressures
   - Standard temperature range down to -29°C (-20°F) without impact testing (and below -29°C when qualified per Code impact requirements)

---

### Types of Power Plant Piping
Power piping systems associated with boilers are classified into three primary categories:

1. **Boiler Proper Piping (BPP):**
   - Internal piping or piping directly connected to the boiler without intervening valves (e.g., internal risers/downcomers, superheater/reheater tubes).
   - **Scope:** Covered entirely by **ASME BPVC Section I** (not B31.1).

2. **Boiler External Piping (BEP):**
   - External piping attached to power boilers (steam/vapor > 15 psig [100 kPa]) and high-temperature water boilers (> 160 psig [1,100 kPa] or > 121°C [250°F]), extending up to and including the second stop valve (or required isolation limits).
   - Includes: Feedwater, main steam, vent, drain, blow-off, and chemical feed piping.
   - **Scope:** Design and construction follow **ASME B31.1**, while administrative oversight, Authorized Inspection (AI), and Code Stamping follow **ASME BPVC Section I**.

3. **Non-Boiler External Piping (NBEP):**
   - All remaining power plant piping downstream/upstream of the BEP boundary limits.
   - **Scope:** Covered entirely by **ASME B31.1** (Section I does not apply).

---

### Exclusions (Not Within the Scope of ASME B31.1)
- **Boiler Proper Piping** (governed exclusively by ASME BPVC Section I)
- Pressure vessels, heat exchangers, and pumps (covered by ASME BPVC or API/ISO standards)
- Low-pressure building heating and distribution systems:
  - Steam and condensate piping ≤ 15 psig [100 kPa (gage)]
  - Hot water heating systems ≤ 30 psig [200 kPa (gage)]
- Fuel gas piping inside buildings (governed by NFPA 54 / ASME B31.2)
- Plumbing, service water, and sewer piping systems governed by local plumbing codes
- Piping for hydraulic or pneumatic tools downstream of the first block valve
- Marine and offshore installations under federal/naval control
- Civil structures (towers, building frames, foundations, and soil support)

---

### Certification Marks & Designators (for BEP)
Stamping and certification rules apply specifically to Boiler External Piping (BEP) under an ASME Quality Control System:
- **S** : Power Boiler Designator (authorizes BEP + Boiler fabrication)
- **A** : Boiler Assembly Designator (authorizes field assembly + BEP)
- **PP** : Pressure Piping Designator (authorizes BEP fabrication/erection only)
